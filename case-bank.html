All work Bank Windhoek · 2021 — 2024

Opening a bank account without the paperwork.

As the sole product designer, I redesigned Bank Windhoek's customer onboarding from scratch: replacing slow, manual processes with digital identity checks and a consistent experience across staff tools, the website and in-branch kiosks.

Role
Senior Product Designer (sole designer)
Timeline
Sep 2021 — Aug 2024
Platform
Staff platform (AppWorks), responsive web, kiosk
Industry
Fintech · Banking
Tools
Figma, Illustrator, After Effects, Miro, Azure DevOps
At a glance

Opening an account at Bank Windhoek meant manual steps, slow software and a trip to a branch. I led the design end to end, from research to a new design system, to make onboarding faster for customers and simpler for the staff serving them.

30%Faster customer onboarding
The problem

Slow for customers, frustrating for staff.

Onboarding was a problem for the business and its users. Outdated, slow software and manual methods made everyday tasks time-consuming for employees and customers alike.

  • Manual, complex and time-consuming processes and workflows.
  • An outdated, inconsistent interface that was hard to use.
  • Customers had to visit a branch to open accounts, manage products or update their profile.
My role

Sole product designer and design advocate.

I was the only product designer, working across several cross-functional agile teams for different product offerings, with members from business, product and development.

  • User research
  • Wireframes
  • Multi-channel UI design
  • Prototypes
  • Animated illustration library
Constraints

A fixed platform and tight timelines.

The staff interface was built on the AppWorks platform, which limited what could be developed. Combined with tight project timelines, that narrowed what was feasible to build, so designs had to work within the platform rather than around it.

Process

Four phases, with the last one never finished.

  1. 01Research
  2. 02Ideate
  3. 03Design
  4. 04Maintenance & rework
01 · Research

Understand today's process before changing it.

I examined the bank's current processes, workflows and interface to find what needed attention and where the biggest opportunities were.

Problem statements

Defined the problems for the business, bank staff and customers.

User feedback

Gathered feedback from the bank's existing customers and staff.

Competitor analysis

Studied how other African banks onboard their customers.

User types

Mapped six user types, from students and parents to people moving banks, with their needs and pain points.

Platform limits

Learned the constraints of the AppWorks platform before designing for it.

UI trends

Reviewed current UI trends and patterns in banking.

02 · Ideate

Map every step, including the ones that fail.

I ran workshops with the team to understand business requirements, features and product specs. I then mapped each stage of onboarding in user flows, including alternate paths and success and failure states, and turned them into low-fidelity wireframes.

03 · Design

A new visual language, built as a system.

UI style

Built a fresh visual language on top of the existing brand and Material Design guidelines.

Accessible colour

Led a review of how colour was used digitally, following UX best practice and accessibility standards.

Design system

Created UI components and guidelines for reuse and consistency across digital platforms.

Illustration library

Created a custom illustration style and library for the UI, and animated it to add moments of delight.

High-fidelity screens

Turned the wireframes into polished screens in the new visual language.

Prototypes

Built working prototypes to show both the functionality and the experience.

What shaped the design

Platform limits

Stayed within AppWorks and avoided heavily customised UI that might not be buildable.

Design principles

Prioritised familiarity, clarity and user control throughout.

Material Design

Built the base design on Material Design components and guidelines.

User feedback

Used feedback to set the hierarchy and priority of information and features.

04 · Maintenance & rework

Keeping the system alive.

This phase never ends. I maintain the design system, revisit UX or UI based on user and business feedback, and extend features as new requirements come in.

The solution

Faster onboarding on every channel.

Research and user feedback solved the core business and user problems, and surfaced insights that improved the product and the bank's workflows too.

Outcome

Customers onboarded faster.

30%Reduction in customer onboarding time
What I learned
  • Building from scratch, alone. I'd been a sole designer before, but never had to create everything from nothing. I guided the process end to end and kept advocating for users.
  • Freedom has its own challenges. Leading with little design input or support showed me that full creative freedom is harder than it sounds, and how much the research phase matters.
