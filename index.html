About me

A little more about me.

When I'm not designing, you'll probably find me exploring Amsterdam by bike, trying out a new coffee spot, or getting lost in a sketchbook. I studied Communication & Multimedia Design and have been curious about how people interact with technology ever since. I believe good design happens where craft, empathy and a bit of curiosity meet — and I'm always up for a conversation about any of the three.

How I work

Design that starts with the problem, not the pixels.

I'm a product designer with 7+ years of experience across fintech, proptech and B2B SaaS. I work end‑to‑end — research, UX flows, UI design and the design systems that keep teams shipping fast without losing quality. I care about the details, but never at the expense of the bigger picture.

01

Research‑informed

Every decision is traceable back to a user problem, a metric, or a constraint — not a trend.

02

Systems thinking

I design components and patterns, not one‑off screens, so products stay consistent as they scale.

03

Craft over noise

Typography, spacing, motion and copy get the same attention as the core flow.

04

Close to engineering

I design in the browser as much as in Figma, and pair closely with the devs who ship the work.
